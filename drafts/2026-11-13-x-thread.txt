A 429 is feedback, not a signal to retry every tenant or switch models blindly.

For a multi-tenant AI support or SaaS analytics workload:
- Parse Retry-After (seconds or HTTP date)
- Reduce concurrency for the affected tenant/route
- Defer work without occupying a worker
- Respect the end-to-end deadline and retry budget
- Treat timeouts as unknown outcomes, not confirmed failures
